# Druid Class Line

kRO's newest job line comes to your own Ragnarok Offline server: Novice → Druid → Karnos → Alitea. 🐺🦅✨

🧬 The new jobs:
🌱 Druid (Novice, Job 10): shapeshifts into a beast, and calls on the three Truths of nature.
🌩️ Karnos (Druid, Base 99 / Job 70): bigger claws, sharper feathers, thunder Charges and earth Growth.
🌟 Alitea (Karnos, Base 200 / Job 70): the 4th-tier job, with AP and the POW / SPL / CON trait stats.
👶 Baby Druid and Baby Karnos are in too.

🐺 Werewolf & 🦅 Wereraptor:
🔄 Shapeshift: Werewolf turns you into a wolf for melee claws and fangs (STR).
🪶 Shapeshift: Wereraptor turns you into a raptor for feathers, speed and ranged hits (DEX).
🧍 In human form, the Truth of Ice ❄️, Wind 🌪️ and Earth 🌱 power your magic (INT).
👀 The transformation shows on screen: your body really changes into the wolf or the raptor.

⚔️ All 84 skills
✅ Every Druid, Karnos and Alitea skill works, from No Mercy Claw to Gravity Hole.
🎆 kRO's own effects and sounds play for every skill: Ice Pillar, Thundering Call, Glacier Monolith, Terra Wave, Furious Storm…
🧊 Ice Pillar and Glacial Monolith stay on the ground for as long as they last.
📖 Skill names, descriptions and the skill tree are in English.

🧙 Job changes at the Job Master
🏛️ The Job Master in Prontera (157, 195) now offers the whole Druid line. No extra NPC needed.
🪓 New Druids get an Axe, and new Karnos a Two-Handed Axe.

💠 Alitea gets AP: the AP bar shows, and Alitea earns trait stat points (POW, SPL, CON…) like every other 4th job.
🔁 Made an Alitea on 1.1.0? Reset its stats once (Reset Girl or @resetstat) to collect the trait points it missed.
📘 GUIDE.md: every job and skill, with what each one needs.

📦 What you need
🍎 Ragnarok Offline 1.6.2 on a Mac, in Renewal mode.
🇰🇷 A kRO data.grf (2026 or later) set as the Fallback GRF in Settings → Advanced. The Druid sprites and effects come from it.

🛠️ Installing
Quit the game.
Unzip druid-class-v1.2.0.zip, open Terminal in the druid-class folder and run ./install.sh.
Start the app. The first start reloads the server, which takes a minute or two. ⏳
Talk to the Job Master and pick your path. 🌳
🔄 After an app update, run ./install.sh again. Updates replace the game's server and client. 🧹 Changed your mind? ./uninstall.sh puts everything back. Switch your Druids to another job first.
